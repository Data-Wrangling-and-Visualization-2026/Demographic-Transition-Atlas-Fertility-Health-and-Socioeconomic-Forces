How to read this canvas

This is not an admin console—it is a research canvas: the globe shows your chosen indicator for a single year. Color scale highlights contrasts. Hover for exact values; click a country to open its timeline, context series, and policy events.

    What policy events are

    Rows in fact_context_event are context anchors, not random news: health reforms, family-planning programs, demographic policy, sometimes shocks and hinge years. They help connect chart lines to real decisions—illustratively, not as proven causality.

    API: /countries, /indicators, /timeseries, /events, /map, /db/stats. Serve this page from the backend (e.g. http://localhost:8000/) so requests share the same origin.
